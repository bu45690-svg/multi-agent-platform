# Machine Learning Concepts

## Transformer Models
Transformer models use self-attention mechanisms to process sequences.
They consist of encoder and decoder blocks with multi-head attention layers.
BERT and GPT are examples of transformer-based models.

## Neural Networks
Neural networks are composed of layers of interconnected nodes.
Backpropagation is used to train neural networks by adjusting weights.

## RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)
RAG combines retrieval systems with language models.
It retrieves relevant documents and uses them as context for generation.
